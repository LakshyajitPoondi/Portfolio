Chennai based creator

This all started when my dad introduced me to my first camera in 2017. He taught me the basics of how a camera works and how a person goes about taking photos. During this time, I used to take pictures of my birds just to practice but I wasn’t particularly interested in it.

The main change came in 2021 when I came across a video of Sudhir Shivaram showcasing his stunning wildlife pictures. I was blown away by how striking the photos were and wanted to take photos like him. This time I got the camera which was a 50D at that time and started practicing photography. I used to binge watch camera techniques and editing tutorials for hours at a time.

When I didn’t have access to a camera, I would use any phone or camera I could get my hands on at that instant and capture that moment in time. I also used to coerce my friends to be models for me which allowed me to gain valuable experience while not having constraints of a proper shoot.

Fast forward a years from 2021 and now I feel like I have come a long way as a photographer. My taste in photos and editing has changed drastically for the better. From cranking saturation and clarity on every photo to editing for hours on a single photo to make it perfect, my editing has some level of refinement now.

This website is a showcase for my work as a photographer. I hope y’all enjoy the photos as much as I did while taking them.

Lakshyajit (lxy visuals)
